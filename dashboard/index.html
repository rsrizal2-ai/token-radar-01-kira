Token launch radar

Which protocol should you farm next?

Every protocol in the snapshot that records no token — and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades — ranked by the implied value of its launch. The value follows from the snapshot's own figures: TVL, seven-day growth, time running with no token, and chain reach, priced against the protocols whose tokens already trade.

Read it honestly. “Records no token” means the snapshot's DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol — it is not proof a protocol has no token. Implied values are this radar's own model, not a prediction that a token will launch, or when, or at what price. Farm plans are starting points: check the protocol's own pages before you move funds, and never pay to claim.
